Teaching resource layer

Native Science PowerPoint Courseware

This is the part designed to remain dependable even when the internet is unavailable. It is not intended to be absorbed into the IED website or InstructOS database.

Why it exists

Offline-first classroom presentation

The Science lessons are produced as native PowerPoint files with controlled navigation, progressive reveal, bilingual support, vocabulary panels, highlighting, full-screen visuals and teacher-friendly presentation behavior.

How it connects

  • IED / Science web: organizes and shares Science content and resources.
  • InstructOS: can eventually know which class and lesson the teacher is working on and launch the right resource.
  • PowerPoint: remains the actual native presentation artifact and does not require the lesson website to be online.

For this review

The full native PowerPoint library is not published on this public review URL. Stuart can open or share an approved deck directly if you want to inspect the presentation mechanics.
