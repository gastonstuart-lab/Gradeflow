The feedback I actually want

You do not need to review every feature. These four questions would be especially useful from both a teacher and a builder.

1. Which parts would you genuinely use during a normal school week?
2. Which areas feel unnecessarily duplicated or confusing?
3. What should be tightly connected, and what should stay separate?
4. Which interface feels fastest and clearest while actually teaching?
